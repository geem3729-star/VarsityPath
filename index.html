About VarsityPath

Empowering South African students to navigate their future.

Our Mission

VarsityPath was built for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026. Our goal is to solve a critical issue in South Africa: the overwhelming and fragmented process of applying to higher education institutions.

We provide a centralized platform where students can explore all 26 public universities, private colleges, and TVET institutions, calculate their APS score with precision, and access essential funding and wellness resources—all in one place.

What We Offer

  • Advanced APS Calculator: Tailored for specific university scoring systems.
  • Comprehensive Directory: Direct links to prospectuses and application portals.
  • Funding Guidance: NSFAS, bursaries, and cost-planning tools.
  • Student Support: Mental health, academic persistence, and accessibility resources.
